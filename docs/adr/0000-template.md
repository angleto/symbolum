# NNNN. Title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN | revises NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What was true that made this a decision rather than an obvious step. Constraints, measurements,
the thing that failed. If there was no pressure, there was no decision, and this is a note
rather than a record.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense and the active voice.

## Consequences

What follows, including what gets worse. A record listing only benefits is a proposal.

## Alternatives rejected

Required, and the section that earns the file. Each alternative with the reason it lost.

An empty version of this section reading "none were considered" is a completed form, not a
record: without it, the next person who finds the rejected option attractive re-litigates the
decision from scratch, and the rejected option is usually attractive, which is why it needed
rejecting.
