# The lemmas, listed

**EXCERPT — for anyone scanning the index:**
Every named theorem the standard chain holds, printed in the theory's own notation, grouped by the
development that holds it and by whether it was assumed or derived. It is a listing and not a
tutorial: to use one of these, see [proving.md](proving.md). The listing below the marker is
generated by `sbt "theories/runMain garden.xeno.symbolum.theories.Lemmas"` and pinned by `LibrarySpec`, so a
lemma added and not listed here fails the build and the failure names that command.

*If you modify this file and this excerpt no longer accurately describes its content, you must
update it.*

## Why this file exists

To use a lemma here you had to know the `String` key of the map it lives in, and there are nine such
maps across `modules/theories` with no listing anywhere. Lean has `exact?`, `apply?`, `rw?`, Loogle
and Moogle; HOL Light has `search`. At this size the cheap answer dominates the expensive one: the
whole library fits on a page, so printing it is most of what a reader needs. `Library.matching`
searches by the shape of a conclusion and is tens of lines on top of the matcher that already
existed.

**Three names are held by two developments each**, and the listing shows both rather than picking
one: `ADD_POSITIVE`, `POSITIVE_NOT_ZERO` and `ZERO_LT_ONE` are `Positivity`'s and are re-exported by
`Order`. A lookup returning the first would have picked by map iteration order, which is a coin toss
dressed as an answer.

**Assumed and derived are labelled and not merged.** A reader searching for what they have about
`x + y` wants `ADD_SYM` whether it was assumed or derived; what the axioms must not do is disappear
into the same bucket as the theorems.

**Every statement below reads back.** Each line is printed and then parsed again through the
signature it was printed with, and what is listed is a form that came back as the theorem it names,
so a line can be pasted into `Ask` or `Find` and is the claim it appears to be. Most are printed
plainly; two carry a type on a free name, because `PROD_ABS_REP` and `WORD_ABS_REP` are equations
about a variable and nothing else in them says what that variable is. A statement no form of which
reads back would be listed with `[not readable back]` in front of it rather than silently, and
`NotationRoundTripSpec` asserts over all of them that none is. Fourteen were unreadable until
2026-09-12, which is what that test exists to stop: the printer wrote `prod(a, b)` where the parser
reads `prod('a, 'b)`.

**Nothing here feeds a rewriter**, deliberately. A union that handed a rule set to `Ring` would mix
theorems from developments with different axioms, which is the case `docs/adr/0008`'s constant origin
covers for defined constants and does not cover for declared ones. A caller that wants to rewrite
with a lemma asks the development that owns it, and `Library.from` is how it finds which that is.

## How to regenerate it

```
sbt "theories/runMain garden.xeno.symbolum.theories.Lemmas"
```

Everything above the marker below is written by hand and is left alone; everything between the
markers is replaced. `LibrarySpec` compares the two and fails naming that command, which is the
whole arrangement: the document breaks exactly when somebody adds a lemma, and that is the moment
they have least appetite for working out how it is produced.
