FOURIER ANALYSIS FINDINGS:

1. ✓ For all tested values, collatz(n, k) converges to the 1↔2 oscillation

2. ✓ The oscillation has period 2, corresponding to frequency ω = π

3. ✓ In the frequency domain, the oscillation appears as:
     - DC component (f=0): magnitude ≈ 1.5 (average value)
     - Fundamental (f=N/2): magnitude ≈ 0.5 (oscillation amplitude)

4. ✓ Before reaching 1: spectrum is complex with many frequencies
     After reaching 1: spectrum simplifies to DC + fundamental only

5. ✓ The Fourier analysis confirms the convergence to periodic behavior

6. ⚠ HOWEVER: This does NOT prove the conjecture for all n
     We've only verified for tested values, not all positive integers

7. 💡 The Fourier approach reveals the periodic structure clearly
     and provides a new lens for analyzing Collatz dynamics
